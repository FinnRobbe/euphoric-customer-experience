# AI writing tells (checklist)

Use these signs when auditing and rewriting employee-facing copy. Flag matches; rewrite to remove the pattern without changing factual meaning.

- Hyphen (-) overuse — especially in unnecessary compound phrases.
- Repetitive structure — sentences follow the same predictable pattern.
- Generic openings — “In today’s fast-paced world…”
- Overused buzzwords — “delve,” “leverage,” “tapestry,” “transformative.”
- Excessive signposting — frequent “Furthermore” and “Moreover.”
- Formulaic contrasts — “It’s not just X; it’s Y.”
- Vague claims — confident statements with few concrete examples.
- Unnaturally polished tone — consistently formal, smooth, and impersonal.
- Redundant conclusions — repeating everything already said.
- Forced balance — giving every viewpoint equal weight without taking a position.

## Repo-specific smells (also flag)

- Em dash overuse (`—`) where a period or short clause would read more naturally
- Filler hedges stacked in every sentence (“typically”, “subject to your scheme rules”, “where clinically appropriate”) when one clear caveat already exists nearby
- Identical FAQ / access-step cadence copied across feature packs
