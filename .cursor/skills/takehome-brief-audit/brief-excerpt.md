## Task focus

Design and prototype an **AI-native flexible benefits experience** within Navigator for a **UK PMI** benefit (Bupa / AXA / Vitality-style complexity):

- cover levels
- dependants
- optional extras
- employee contribution tradeoffs

Employee experience only. No administrator workflows.

## Part 1 deliverables (score these)

The prototype should:

1. Demonstrate a clear understanding of flexible benefits and the types of changes an employee might make to a UK PMI benefit.
2. Feel like it belongs within the Navigator product (consumer-grade, beautifully designed employee experience).
3. Use the current benefit page as a starting point; creative UI/UX changes are allowed.
4. Be presentable and walkable — reviewed together in the interview.
